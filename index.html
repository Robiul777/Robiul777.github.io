About Me

I am Md Robiul Islam, a Graduate Research Assistant in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Tennessee State University (TSU), Nashville, TN, where I am completing my MS in Computer and Information Systems Engineering. I presented my MS thesis this spring, completed all coursework, and will submit my thesis book this summer.

My long-term goal is to contribute to intelligent manufacturing systems by combining domain knowledge from apparel production with modern computational techniques such as deep learning, image analysis, and contrastive learning.

Selected Projects

Fabric Defect Detection Using Computer Vision

Developed a conceptual machine learning pipeline for detecting defects in textile fabrics using image-based features. The project explores how CNN-based models can support automated quality control in apparel manufacturing.

Tools: Python, OpenCV, TensorFlow/PyTorch, Image Processing

Apparel Manufacturing Process Analysis

Analyzed garment production workflow, quality control points, and operational bottlenecks in apparel manufacturing. The work focused on improving productivity, reducing defects, and supporting data-driven decision-making.

Areas: Production Planning, Quality Control, Lean Manufacturing

Research Review on AI in Textile Manufacturing

Reviewed recent applications of machine learning and computer vision in textile inspection, smart manufacturing, sustainability, and automation.

Areas: Literature Review, AI Applications, Smart Manufacturing
