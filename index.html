About Me

I am Md Robiul Islam, an Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE) PhD student at the University of South Florida (USF) and a Graduate Research Assistant under Dr. Hayder Al-Hraishawi, Assistant Professor, Dept. of ECE, USF, Tampa, Florida. I completed MS in Computer and Information Systems Engineering (CISE) under the dept. of ECE from Tennessee State University (TSU) where I was working as a Graduate Research Assistant under Dr. Sagnika Ghosh, Associate Professor, Dept. of ECE, Tennessee State University (TSU), Nashville, TN. I completed BSc in Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE) from Pabna University of Science and Technology (PUST), Pabna, Bangladesh where I was working as an Undergraduate Research Assistant under Dr. Md. Feroz Ali, Associate Professor, Dept. of EEE, PUST, Pabna, Bangladesh.

My research agenda focuses on the development of intelligent and autonomous communication frameworks for next-generation wireless networks, with an emphasis on artificial intelligence (AI)-enabled 5G/6G systems, non-terrestrial networks (NTN), and satellite communications. I investigate the integration of machine learning (ML), deep reinforcement learning (DRL), and multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) techniques into wireless communication systems to address complex challenges in dynamic resource allocation, spectrum optimization, adaptive beamforming, network control, and intelligent network orchestration. My research interests extend to RF communication systems, wireless channel modeling, massive MIMO, millimeter-wave (mmWave) communications, satellite-terrestrial integrated networks, and space-air-ground integrated networks (SAGIN). Through AI-driven approaches, my goal is to design reliable, adaptive, and self-optimizing communication architectures for future 6G networks, IoT ecosystems, autonomous systems, and mission-critical applications.

My technical foundation is built on intelligent wireless communication system design, including RF communication systems, digital modulation techniques, OFDM-based systems, MIMO and massive MIMO architectures, beamforming, wireless channel modeling, and spectrum-efficient network design. My current research involves applying artificial intelligence and machine learning techniques, including deep reinforcement learning (DRL) algorithms such as PPO, DDPG, SAC, DQN, TD3, and multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL), to address complex challenges in next-generation wireless networks. These challenges include dynamic resource allocation, adaptive beamforming, spectrum management, network optimization, and autonomous control for 5G/6G systems, non-terrestrial networks (NTN), and satellite-terrestrial integrated networks. I have experience with machine learning and deep learning approaches, including ensemble learning, neural networks, CNN/RNN-based architectures, transformers, and attention mechanisms, for intelligent communication system modeling, prediction, and optimization.

My research experience combines advanced artificial intelligence techniques with communication system design and optimization. I am proficient in Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow, Keras, MATLAB/Simulink, Stable-Baselines3, and OpenAI Gym for developing and evaluating AI-driven wireless communication solutions. My hands-on experience includes implementing deep reinforcement learning environments, developing optimization frameworks, and conducting simulation-based analysis for intelligent network control, resource allocation, and autonomous decision-making. I also have experience with cloud computing platforms, including AWS, GCP, and Azure, supporting scalable AI model development and experimental workflows. These technical skills enable me to bridge theoretical communication principles with practical AI-driven solutions for next-generation wireless networks.

My peer-reviewed publications across IEEE Access, IEEE NAPS, IEEE ICECIE, IEEE QPAIN, IEEE SPICSCON, and the International Journal of Applied Mathematics demonstrate consistent research contributions in AI-driven optimization, anomaly detection, cyber-physical systems, intelligent energy systems, and reinforcement learning-based decision-making. These experiences have strengthened my ability to formulate research problems, develop data-driven solutions, validate computational models, and communicate findings through high-quality scientific publications. Beyond technical research, I bring strong collaboration and leadership experience as a Graduate Research Assistant, IEEE Associate Member, and Secretary of the South Asian Student Association (SASA) at Tennessee State University. My current research vision focuses on advancing intelligent wireless communication systems, including AI-native 5G/6G networks, non-terrestrial networks (NTN), satellite communications, and distributed multi-agent systems, toward reliable, adaptive, and autonomous connectivity solutions.

Selected Projects

Deep Reinforcement Learning for Intelligent Decision-Making

Worked with deep reinforcement learning algorithms such as PPO, DDPG, SAC, DQN, TD3, and MARL to analyze intelligent decision-making under uncertainty. The project focus includes algorithm comparison, policy optimization, and performance evaluation in simulated environments.

Tools: Python, PyTorch, Stable-Baselines3, OpenAI Gym, MATLAB/Simulink

AI and Machine Learning for Smart Systems

Applied classical machine learning, deep learning, and hybrid AI methods to real-world intelligent system problems. The work includes supervised and unsupervised learning, model evaluation, feature engineering, time-series analysis, and multimodal prediction.

Tools: Python, TensorFlow, Keras, Scikit-learn, XGBoost, Random Forest, SVM

LLM Integration and MCP Server Development

Developed hands-on experience in connecting large language models with enterprise systems through MCP server development, secure authentication, authorization, and real-time REST/WebSocket-based communication.
